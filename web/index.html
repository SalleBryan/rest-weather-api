Up to 10 requests per minute and 100 per UTC day in this browser, shared across location searches and forecasts. Forecasts are cached for 15 minutes; location matches for 24 hours. Suggestions appear after three characters and a short typing pause. No background polling or automatic retries.

Explore uses Open-Meteo’s free, non-commercial endpoints, without an API key or billing account. Your own API has a separate browser allowance and connects only when you choose.
